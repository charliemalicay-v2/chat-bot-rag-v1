# Northwind Outfitters FAQ

## Store hours and locations

Our flagship store in Portland, Oregon is open Monday to Saturday from 10 am to 8 pm and Sunday from 11 am to 6 pm. The online shop is open around the clock. Customer support answers email and chat from 8 am to 6 pm Pacific time, Monday to Friday, and replies to most messages within one business day.

## Gear rentals

You can rent tents, sleeping bags, backpacks and stoves from the Portland store by the day or the week. Rentals require a valid photo ID and a refundable deposit equal to half the retail price of the item. A weekend rental of a two-person tent costs $35. Rental gear must be returned clean and dry, or a $15 cleaning fee applies.

## Price matching

We match the advertised price of any authorised retailer on the same item in stock, within 14 days of your purchase. Send us the link and your order number. Clearance items, final sale items and marketplace sellers are excluded from price matching.

## Gift cards and loyalty

Gift cards never expire and can be used online or in store. Our Trailhead rewards programme is free to join and earns one point per dollar spent. Every 500 points become a $25 reward certificate. Members also get early access to seasonal sales and free shipping on all orders over $50.

## Trip planning help

Our staff have hiked, climbed and paddled all over the Pacific Northwest, and they offer free gear consultations in store and by video call. Book a 20-minute slot online and tell us where you are going, the season and your budget, and we will recommend a kit list.

## Recycling old gear

Bring worn-out tents, packs and jackets to any store during the first week of each month. We send them to a textile recycler and give you a 10 percent discount voucher for your next purchase.
