# Northwind Lifetime Warranty

## What is covered

Every Northwind-branded product carries a lifetime warranty against defects in materials and workmanship. If a zipper fails, a seam splits under normal use, a buckle snaps or a pole section cracks without a clear impact, we will repair the item or replace it at our discretion. The warranty covers the original purchaser and lasts for the useful life of the product.

Third-party brands sold in our shop, such as stoves, headlamps and water filters made by other companies, follow the manufacturer's own warranty. The manufacturer's terms are printed on the product page. We will help you file the claim if you contact us with your order number.

## What is not covered

The warranty does not cover normal wear and tear, such as worn-down boot soles, faded fabric or the gradual loss of loft in a sleeping bag after many years of use. It also does not cover damage caused by accidents, misuse, unauthorised repairs, fire, or storing gear wet or compressed for long periods. Cosmetic marks, scuffs and stains are not defects.

## How to make a claim

Email support with your order number, a short description of the problem and two or three clear photos. If we need to inspect the item, we will send you a prepaid shipping label. Most claims are decided within 7 business days of the item arriving at our repair centre.

If the item cannot be repaired and has been discontinued, we will offer a replacement of equal value or store credit for the current retail price. Repairs that are not covered by the warranty can often still be done for a small fee. Ask us for a quote before you send anything in.
