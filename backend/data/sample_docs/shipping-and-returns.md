# Shipping and Returns Policy

## Shipping

Northwind Outfitters ships within the United States and Canada. Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days and costs $6.95. Orders over $75 qualify for free standard shipping. Expedited shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs $19.95. Orders placed before 2 pm Pacific time on a business day leave our warehouse the same day.

We do not ship stove fuel canisters by air. Isobutane fuel canisters travel by ground only, which means delivery to Alaska and Hawaii is not available for those items. Oversized items such as four-person tents and canoe paddles may add a $12 handling surcharge, which is shown at checkout before you pay.

You will receive a tracking number by email as soon as your order ships. If a package has not arrived 10 business days after the ship date, contact support and we will open a carrier investigation and send a replacement if the package is lost.

## Returns

You can return any unused item within 60 days of delivery for a full refund to your original payment method. Items must be in new condition, in their original packaging, with all tags attached. Tents must be returned unpitched or only lightly pitched indoors, with no dirt, no odor and no stakes stuck in the ground.

To start a return, sign in to your account, open the order and choose Return Items to print a prepaid label. Returns are free for orders shipped within the United States. Canadian customers pay the return postage. Refunds are issued within 5 business days after we receive the parcel.

Fuel canisters, opened water filters, underwear and socks cannot be returned for hygiene and safety reasons. Final sale items are marked clearly on the product page and are not eligible for return or exchange.

## Exchanges

To swap a size or colour, return the original item and place a new order. We will refund the original purchase, and you can keep your place in the stock queue by ordering the replacement first.
